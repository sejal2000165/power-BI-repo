Automobile Customer Analytics

Purpose
This dashboard analyzes customer behavior, preferences, income levels, and purchase intentions for automobile sales.

Filters (Slicers)
Users can filter data by:
Customer ID
City
Fuel Preference
Preferred Car Segment
Gender
Monthly Income Slab
Marital Status

KPI Cards
The dashboard contains key metrics such as:
Total Customers
Dealer Visits
Test Drive Completed
Average Family Size
Average Budget
Average Purchase Probability
EMI Eligible Customers

Charts Used
Pie Chart
Customer distribution by Monthly Income Slab.
Column Chart
Customer distribution by Residence type.
Donut Chart
Budget distribution by Fuel Preference.
Waterfall Chart

Business Insights
Identifies which income groups contain the most potential customers.
Shows fuel preferences (Petrol, Diesel, EV, etc.).
Helps understand customer budgets and financing eligibility.
Tracks dealer visits and test drives to measure sales funnel performance.
Supports targeted marketing based on demographics and purchase timelines.
Overall Assessment
This is a single-page customer analytics dashboard designed for automobile dealerships to understand customer profiles, buying behavior, and sales opportunities through interactive filters and visualizations.
