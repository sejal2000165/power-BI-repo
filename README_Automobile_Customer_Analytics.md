# 🚗 Automobile Customer Analytics Dashboard

## 📌 Overview
The **Automobile Customer Analytics Dashboard** is an interactive, single-page business intelligence solution designed for automobile dealerships. It helps analyze customer demographics, purchasing behavior, income profiles, vehicle preferences, and sales funnel performance to support data-driven decision-making.

---

## 🎯 Purpose
This dashboard enables dealerships and sales teams to:

- Understand customer demographics and buying preferences.
- Identify high-potential customer segments.
- Monitor dealership engagement and test-drive activity.
- Analyze customer budgets and financing eligibility.
- Improve targeted marketing and sales conversion strategies.

---

## 🔍 Interactive Filters (Slicers)

Users can dynamically filter the dashboard using:

| Filter | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| Customer ID | Analyze individual customer records |
| City | View customer trends by location |
| Fuel Preference | Petrol, Diesel, EV, Hybrid, etc. |
| Preferred Car Segment | Hatchback, Sedan, SUV, Luxury, etc. |
| Gender | Male / Female / Other |
| Monthly Income Slab | Income-based customer segmentation |
| Marital Status | Married / Single analysis |

---

## 📊 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

The dashboard highlights critical business metrics:

| KPI | Description |
|------|-------------|
| Total Customers | Total customer records available |
| Dealer Visits | Number of dealership visits |
| Test Drive Completed | Customers who completed test drives |
| Average Family Size | Average family size of customers |
| Average Budget | Average vehicle purchase budget |
| Average Purchase Probability | Likelihood of purchase |
| EMI Eligible Customers | Customers eligible for financing |

## 📈 Visualizations Used

### 🥧 Pie Chart
**Customer Distribution by Monthly Income Slab**
- Shows the proportion of customers across income categories.
- Helps identify high-value customer segments.

### 📊 Column Chart
**Customer Distribution by Residence Type**
- Compares customer counts across residence categories.
- Supports demographic analysis.

### 🍩 Donut Chart
**Budget Distribution by Fuel Preference**
- Displays spending patterns by preferred fuel type.
- Reveals budget differences between Petrol, Diesel, EV, and Hybrid buyers.

### 🌊 Waterfall Chart
**Customer Count by Purchase Timeline with Test Drive Information**
- Tracks customer progression across purchase stages.
- Highlights the impact of test drives on purchase intent.

## 💡 Business Insights

### Customer Segmentation
- Identifies income groups with the highest purchasing potential.

- Understands customer spending capacity.
- Highlights EMI-eligible customers for financing campaigns.

### Sales Funnel Performance
- Monitors dealership visits and test-drive completion rates.
- Identifies conversion opportunities.

### Targeted Marketing
- Supports campaigns based on demographics, location, income, and purchase timeline.
- Improves customer engagement and lead quality.
